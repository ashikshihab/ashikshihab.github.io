About
Me

Hi, I'm Ashik. I'm a product designer in Kochi.

I came to design from electronics and communication engineering, which is probably why I start from how a system works before how a screen looks. I joined Turbolab as a visual designer, moved into product, and four years later it's all been B2B SaaS. Analytics platforms, marketplaces, data products.

I design from evidence, not opinion. When there's no direct line to users, I go to the people closest to them. I'd rather ship a reversible bet and read what happens than win an argument.

I build design systems so the work scales past me, and I lead a small team through the reviews that keep those systems honest. What interests me most is the reasoning underneath a design: the tradeoff taken, the option rejected, and why. The case studies are where that lives.

Right now I'm looking for my next product design role, somewhere with harder problems and people to learn from. If that sounds like your team, let's connect or drop me a line.
